Recommended format

Instagram Reel Cover Size Standards

A vertical 9:16 image works best for Reel covers. The gold-standard high-quality export size is 1080 × 1920 pixels.

Keep your critical text, face, and brand logos near the center area. Top and bottom areas can be covered by app interface elements, captions, or platform crop variations.

Quick Checklist

  • Use vertical 9:16 format (1080 × 1920 px)
  • Keep key titles inside the green safe zone
  • Verify the 1:1 profile grid square crop
  • Avoid placing faces or text at extreme edges
  • Export high-quality JPG or PNG files

Placement Matters

Safe Zone Anatomy

ReelCoverFit highlights practical unsafe areas so you can avoid placing important content where it gets covered.

Top Area (Unsafe)

Top 250 pixels are obscured by the app status bar, audio attribution, and camera buttons.

Center Area (Safe)

The central zone is visible in both the 9:16 full view and the square 1:1 profile grid.

Bottom Area (Unsafe)

Bottom 360 pixels are covered by creator username, post captions, audio track, and like/comment buttons.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Instagram Reel cover size, safe zones, cropping, and privacy.

What is the best Instagram Reel cover size?

The best Instagram Reel cover size is a vertical 9:16 image. A commonly used high-quality size is 1080 × 1920 pixels.

What does ReelCoverFit check?

ReelCoverFit checks your uploaded Reel cover image dimensions, compares the aspect ratio with 9:16, shows a vertical Reel preview, highlights safe zones, and shows a profile grid crop preview.

Why does my Instagram Reel cover get cropped?

Your Reel cover can appear differently in the full Reel view, profile grid, and thumbnail previews. If your image is not close to 9:16, or if text is placed near the edges, parts of the cover may look cropped or hard to read.

What is a Reel cover safe zone?

A Reel cover safe zone is the area where important elements like text, faces, logos, and titles are less likely to be cut off or hidden. Keeping important content near the center usually gives a cleaner preview.

How do I stop my Reel cover text from getting cut?

Use a 9:16 image and keep your main title, face, logo, or product away from the top, bottom, and extreme side edges. ReelCoverFit helps you preview these safe and unsafe areas before posting.

Does ReelCoverFit resize my Reel cover?

Yes. Upload your cover, drag it inside the 9:16 preview, adjust the zoom, use the creator guides, and download the manually cropped Instagram-ready result.

Does ReelCoverFit upload my image?

No. Your image is processed locally inside your browser. ReelCoverFit does not upload your image to any server.

Can I use this as an Instagram grid preview tool?

Yes, ReelCoverFit includes a simple profile grid crop preview so you can check how the center part of your Reel cover may appear on your Instagram profile grid.

Can I use this for YouTube Shorts covers?

You can use the 9:16 preview as a rough check for vertical content, but ReelCoverFit is currently focused on Instagram Reel cover size and safe zone checking.
